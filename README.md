# Welcome to Imprese Riunite Website

This website is a polished digital presentation developed on behalf of Imprese Riunite to showcase the group’s long-standing expertise and commitment to major infrastructure and technological projects.

It tells the story of a company rooted in a strong construction legacy since 1971, with Costruzioni Angelico leading major public works and Nuova C.C.S. focusing on advanced plant engineering and energy systems. The site highlights:

over 50 years of experience in civil, maritime, hydraulic and institutional construction,
key works for Rome Capitale, the Ministries of Justice and Defense, ENAV and other major public clients,
international landmark projects in Saudi Arabia and strategic infrastructure across Italy,
specialized capabilities in photovoltaic systems, power plants, MT/BT substations, lighting, security and industrial autonomy,
important quality certifications such as ISO and SOA.
Designed for Imprese Riunite, the site emphasizes the company’s history, certifications, flagship projects and the professional team behind its growth, delivering a modern, refined online identity that reflects both heritage and technical excellence.
